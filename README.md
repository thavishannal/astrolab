# Third-Year Astrolab Project
## On Calibrating the Period-Luminosity Relationship of Classical Cepheids
My third-year lab project, in which I constructed a data reduction pipeline to calibrate the Period-Luminosity relationship of classical Cepheids.
Together with my lab partner, I observed 19 target stars over an extended period using Durham University's DRACO 2 telescope.
We constructed an empirical light-curve model that was successfully used to measure the magnitude and period of individual Cepheids despite our sparse data.
